For bands

Submit your band

HeavyMusix Radio plays hand-picked unsigned metal from around the world next to the heavy hitters. Submitting is free, always.

submit@heavymusix.com

We’re just getting started. Submissions are open now and our first review round is being set up. Reviews take time, so please be patient.

What to send

Put it all in one email. Incomplete submissions can’t be reviewed.

  • Band name, city and country
  • Genre (death, thrash, doom, black, deathcore, metalcore, sludge…)
  • Bio: a short paragraph about the band: who you are, when you started, what you sound like, what you’ve released
  • Photos: 2 or 3 good band photos and your logo, as a shared link (Google Drive, Dropbox or your press kit)
  • Music: a full album or EP, or at least 4 to 5 tracks. One single isn’t enough for a review. Links to Bandcamp, Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud
  • Contact: a contact name, email, and your social and merch links
  • Rights: a line confirming the band owns the music and that HeavyMusix may play it in the playlist, streams and clips if you’re selected

Links only, please. No attachments.

How selection works

  • Every submission is listened to. We review the full set of tracks, the bio and the band as a whole. It’s a process, and it takes time.
  • Not every band is selected for HeavyMusix Radio or the stream. We pick bands that fit the sound and quality of the playlist.
  • Please be patient. Reviews can take several weeks. Sending the same submission again doesn’t speed it up.
  • If you’re selected, we’ll email you before anything is added, and tag you when you’re featured.
  • Not selected this time? You’re welcome to submit again when you have new music.
HeavyMusix never charges bands to be played or reviewed. If anyone claiming to be us asks you for money for a spot, it isn’t us.
