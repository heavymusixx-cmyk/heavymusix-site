Privacy

Your info, in plain words

Last updated November 15, 2026. HeavyMusix is a metal radio and news site based in Phoenix, Arizona. Questions or requests: info@heavymusix.com.

What we collect

Browsing heavymusix.com does not require an account, and we do not run ad trackers.

If you email us (band submissions, ideas, questions), we keep your email and what you sent so we can reply and review it.

If you join our email list, we collect your email address and, only if you choose to give them, your first name, favorite metal styles and city. We use these to send you the newsletter, show alerts near you and news in the styles you like. You can unsubscribe from any email with one click.

The music player

HeavyMusix Radio plays through YouTube. When you press play, YouTube (Google) may set cookies and collect data under its own privacy policy.

What we never do

We never sell, rent or share your information with anyone for their marketing. Our email list is stored with our email service provider only so the emails can be sent.

Your choices

Email info@heavymusix.com to see, correct or delete what we have about you, and we will do it promptly. These rights apply wherever you live, including the EU and UK.

Kids

HeavyMusix is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
