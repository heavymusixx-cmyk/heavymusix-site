About

Built by a metalhead for unsigned bands

HeavyMusix started in Phoenix, Arizona, with one playlist and one rule: every band gets a turn.

What we do

  • HeavyMusix Radio: a daily-reshuffled playlist of hand-picked unsigned and underground bands next to the heavy hitters.
  • Band Survival Kit: an animated series and free checklists teaching bands the business side, so they don’t get ripped off. (In production.)
  • News: metal news, Phoenix shows, new bands and new episodes.

What’s coming

Merch, do-it-yourself band tools, a zine featuring hand-picked unsigned bands from around the world, a 24/7 stream, a HeavyMusix compilation, and one day a label that pays its bands fairly.

Who’s behind HeavyMusix

Heavy is the DJ and editor of HeavyMusix: a lifelong metalhead in the Phoenix area who goes to shows, digs through the underground and picks every unsigned band that gets played. HeavyMusix is independent. No label, sponsor or band pays for coverage.

How we write the news

  • Every story is our own writing, researched from the original report, the band or the label. We name the outlet we learned it from at the end of each story.
  • We publish fresh worldwide metal news every morning, plus Arizona shows and a daily look back at metal history. History dates are checked against two sources.
  • Stories are researched and written with the help of AI tools, under Heavy’s rules: real facts only, no copied articles, no lyrics, no rumors passed off as news.
  • Nobody can pay to be covered, reviewed or played.
  • Spotted a mistake? Email info@heavymusix.com and we’ll fix it fast.

Our promise

Fair and honest, across the board

Bands are never charged to be reviewed or played. As HeavyMusix grows into services, promotion and merch, every deal will be in writing, clear and simple, with fair, honest pay for the artists we work with.
