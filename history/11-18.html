November 18 in metal history

Albums, birthdays, band beginnings and big moments in metal on November 18, checked against two sources each.

  • 1949 · BornHappy birthday to Herman Rarebell, longtime drummer of Scorpions through their 1970s and 1980s peak.Source: I Love Heavy Metal Radio, Scorpions history
  • 1985 · AlbumGerman power metal pioneers Helloween release their debut album, "Walls of Jericho", with Kai Hansen on vocals and guitar.Source: This Day in Metal, I Love Heavy Metal Radio
  • 1988 · AlbumManowar release "Kings of Metal", the album that gave the world "Hail and Kill" and "Blood of the Kings".Source: This Day in Metal, I Love Heavy Metal Radio
  • 1997 · AlbumMetallica release "Reload", the companion to 1996's "Load", featuring "The Memory Remains" and "Fuel". Listen on HeavyMusix RadioSource: Ultimate Classic Rock, This Day in Metal
  • 2016 · AlbumMetallica release "Hardwired... to Self-Destruct", their first album in eight years, featuring "Moth Into Flame". Listen on HeavyMusix RadioSource: I Love Heavy Metal Radio, Metallica.com
